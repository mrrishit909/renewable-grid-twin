Renewable Grid Twin

An invented 118-bus network on a windy, sunny day that turns cloudy. A forecast that missed the clouds, a line that trips on the wind export corridor, an N-1 scan, and battery and curtailment dispatch optimised over the DC network, every alternative scored on the true weather. …
The network, its assets, weather, telemetry and prices are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real grid, an IEEE test case or real weather, and the twin that scores the plans is the generator itself. DC power flow only. No outside model or service is called.
